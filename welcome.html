Harbour festival returns after five-year break

The harbour festival returned on Saturday for the first time since 2019, drawing about 8,000 people to the waterfront despite light rain. Stallholders said takings were down on the last event, but most were relieved it went ahead at all. Fireworks were moved to Sunday because of the wind.

In today’s fast-paced world, community events play a crucial role in fostering connection. Moreover, festivals like this one serve as a testament to the vibrant tapestry of local culture. It’s important to note that such gatherings empower residents to navigate the complexities of modern life together.

Organisers hope to make the festival annual again. “We nearly gave up last year,” said volunteer Priya Nair. “Then 40 people turned up to a planning meeting in a pub, and that was that.”
